Bedlam 1.6.0 fixes Android 10 and 11, moves the encrypted DNS presets to provider hostnames, refreshes the icons and updates the Hysteria core to 2.12.3.

**Android 10 and 11**

- A system title bar covered the top of the app. It is gone, and the splash screen shows the icon.
- On Android 10, importing or choosing a profile and changing Apps to proxy closed the app. This is fixed.

**DNS**

- The DNS over TLS and DNS over HTTPS presets use one.one.one.one, cloudflare-dns.com and dns.google instead of numeric addresses, which some servers could not reach. Your server has to resolve these names.
- A DNS connection that stops answering is replaced sooner, and DNS errors in the log say more about what failed.
- Traffic to port 53 that is not DNS no longer goes to the DNS provider.

**Other changes**

- New app icon, and the icons inside the app use Material Symbols.
- The connect button animates until the connection attempt ends.
- The profile editor asks before it drops unsaved edits, hides the obfuscation password and the client key, and offers to reconnect after you save the profile in use.
- The STUN servers field keeps the comma you type, so a realm profile can list more than one server.
- The VPN service stops when you disconnect instead of staying in the background.
- Messages no longer come back after you rotate the screen.
- Settings and Logs do no work while another tab is open.
- The notification permission prompt appears after your first connect, not at launch.
- Screen readers announce the connect button, the profile list and the editor fields.
- The tunnel's network stack and the libraries are updated.
