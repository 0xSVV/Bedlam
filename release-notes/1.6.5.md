Bedlam 1.6.5 keeps DNS working on slow connections, tells you when the VPN stops, and shares profiles as hysteria2:// links.

**DNS on slow connections**

- A DNS over TCP or TLS connection that is slow to open is no longer dropped when a lookup times out. Bedlam finishes opening it, sends several lookups over it and keeps answers that arrive late, so lookups right after connecting succeed much sooner. DNS over HTTPS and DNS over QUIC share one connection instead of opening one per lookup.
- If the first DNS server is slow, Bedlam asks the next one while the first keeps trying, and goes back to the first server after five minutes.
- With Cloudflare, Google or custom DNS, cached answers survive a switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data. Answers that a name does not exist are cached too, for up to five minutes.

**When the VPN stops**

- If the tunnel fails or cannot start, a notification gives the reason and a Reconnect button. Alerts have their own channel, so muting the status notification no longer hides them.
- A VPN that was on turns back on after Bedlam updates.
- When Android turns the VPN off, the alert no longer blames another VPN app.

**Profile links**

- Share link and Copy link on a profile give a hysteria2:// link that other Hysteria apps can import. Bedlam first lists any setting the link leaves out.
- Pasting several links imports each one. Links keep a "+" in the password and accept insecure=true and mport.

**Other changes**

- Debug lines no longer push other lines out of the log. Share log includes every line and can hide addresses.
- Bedlam vibrates on connect only when you start it.
