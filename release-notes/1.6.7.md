Bedlam 1.6.7 makes Home and settings clearer, and starts and scrolls faster.

**Home**

- Navigation tabs always show their labels. On tablets and in landscape, a side rail replaces the bottom bar and Home puts the connection next to your profiles.
- The connection status is now the headline on Home, with the active profile and connected time under it. With no profiles yet, Home offers an Import button.
- Switching profiles while connected stays on Connecting until the new profile starts, without a dark flash around the button.

**Settings**

- Routing shows each setting in its own card, with Direct DNS above Remote DNS. Auto MTU is now 1500 bytes instead of 1280.
- App routing modes have full names and a short explanation of each.
- If you type a number a profile can't use, it stays visible with an error and Save stays off until you fix it. Advanced profile sections stay hidden until you tap Show advanced settings.
- Direct routes have an actions menu, and deleting one offers Undo.

**Other changes**

- Bedlam starts faster, and long logs and route lists scroll with fewer dropped frames.
- The import sheet scrolls above the keyboard, and log timestamps have higher contrast in light mode.
